Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with us.

Do you sell fake followers or bots?

No. We only use real marketing methods — organic growth, content strategy, paid campaigns, and community management. We do not sell fake followers, bots, or engagement that violates platform Terms of Service.

Do you need my account password?

No. We never ask for your password or account access. All growth is done through external promotion and content optimization. You keep full control of your account.

How long until I see results?

Results vary by platform, niche, and content quality. The test service runs for 7 days. Monthly packages typically show measurable progress within 30–60 days. We provide weekly reports so you can track progress.

Can I get a refund?

Service fees are non-refundable once work has started. Crypto payments are final and cannot be refunded. We recommend starting with the test service (₹499) before committing to a monthly package.

Which platforms do you support?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X (Twitter), Telegram, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn. We also offer custom multi-platform campaigns.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI, Razorpay (cards, net banking, wallets), and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB). Crypto clients pay 100% upfront. Other clients pay 50% deposit, 50% after first report.

Do you guarantee follower counts?

No. Platform algorithms control who sees and follows content. We guarantee effort, strategy, transparent reporting, and real marketing — not specific follower numbers.
