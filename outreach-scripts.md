# Cold Outreach Scripts (Anonymous / No-Face Brand)

Use these across Instagram DMs, email, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn.  
**Rule:** Never promise fake followers or bots. Sell real marketing — content strategy, ad management, engagement campaigns, and organic growth.

---

## 1. Instagram DM (Creator / Small Business)

**First message — keep it short, specific, not spammy**

> Hey [Name], I've been following [their page] for a bit — love the [specific post/type of content].
>
> I run a social media growth service. We help [their niche] accounts grow real engagement through content strategy and targeted outreach — no bots, no fake followers.
>
> Open to a quick chat this week? No pressure either way.

**Follow-up (3 days later if no reply)**

> Just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to share a quick case study of a similar account we grew. Let me know if you'd like to see it.

---

## 2. Email Template (Local Businesses / Brands)

> [Brand Name] — Social Growth

## Follow-Up Schedule

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Initial outreach |
| Day 4 | Gentle follow-up ("bumping this") |
| Day 8 | Share a case study or result |
| Day 15 | Final check-in, then move on |

**Never message the same person more than 3 times.**

---

## Anonymous Trust-Building Tactics (No Face / Personal Details)

Since you don't want to be public, build trust with:

1. **Case study screenshots** — blurred-out client handles + "before/after" metrics (engagement rate, follower growth).
2. **Client testimonials** — text only, with first name + handle (get permission).
3. **Professional branding** — clean logo, consistent colors, no personal photos needed.
4. **Clear terms** — refund/rework policy, weekly reporting, no fake-engagement clause.
5. **Communication via WhatsApp/Email only** — never video calls unless you want to.
