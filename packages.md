## How to Present Prices to Clients

1. **Lead with results, not price.** "We grew a similar account from 1K to 8K engaged followers in 60 days."
2. **Offer a trial month.** First month at 20% off if they commit to 3 months.
3. **Never guarantee specific follower counts.** Guarantee effort, strategy, and reporting — platforms control the algorithm.
4. **Get paid upfront.** 50% deposit before starting, 50% after first report (or full monthly upfront for simplicity).
5. **For crypto clients:** Require full payment upfront before starting work (crypto payments are irreversible).
